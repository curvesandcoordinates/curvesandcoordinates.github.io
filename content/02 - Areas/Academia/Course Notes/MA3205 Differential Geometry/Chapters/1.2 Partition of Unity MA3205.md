# Partition of Unity

**Previous**: [[1.1 Tangent Space MA3205]]
**Next Chapter**: [[1.3 Differential of Functions on Manifolds MA3205]]

> [!definition] Partiiton of Unity
> A partition of unity is a set of function $P \subset \mathcal{F}(M)$ with the following properties for all $f \in P$
>
> - $0 \leq f \leq 1$
> - $\sum\limits_{f \in P}f = 1$m
> - $\set{\supp(f) \midline f \in P}$ is lovally finity, meaning that for all $p \in M$ there exists a nbhd that has a non-empty intersection with the supports of only finitely many functions $f$.
>   Note: The second condition already gives us that $\set{ \supp(f) \midline f \in P}$ is a cover of $M$

> [!theorem] Local Finiteness of Spaces with Countable Basis
> Give a manifold $M$ with a countable basis, then any open cover has a locally finite refinement.

`\begin{proof}`
We can assume that our topology has a countable basis $\set{U_i}_{i \in \N}$ where $U_{i}$ have compact closure. Now define $V_{k} := \bigcup_{i =0}^{i_{k}} U_{i}$ with $i_{0} = 0$ and $i_{k} = \min\set{n > i_{k-1} \midline V_{k-1} \subset \bigcup_{i = 0}^{n} U_{i}}$
Now we have that $M \subset \bigcup_{i = 0}^{\infty} V_{k}$ and $\overline{V_{k}} \subset V_{k+1}$ and $\overline{V_{k}}$ is compact for all $k$
Given an open covering $\set{W_{\alpha} \midline \alpha \in A}$ for $M$ one can choose $\Omega_{k}$ as a finite subcover of open cover $\set{W_{\alpha} \cap (V_{k+1} \setminus \overline{V_{k-2}})}$
of $\overline{V_{k}} \setminus V_{k-1}$

Then the set $\bigcup_{k} \Omega$ is locally finite refinement of $\set{W_{\alpha} \midline \alpha \in A}$.
`\end{proof}`

> [!corollary] Partition of Unity, Existence
> For any open cover $D$ of a manifold $M$ (with countable basis) there exists a subordinate partition of unity $P$ (meaning for all $f \in P$ we have $\supp f \subset U$ for some $U \in D$)

`\begin{proof}`
Due to the previous theorem we now that $D$ has a locally finite refinement. We can constrcut bump functions on this refinement as we have shown above.

> The only problem that can occur is that we cant know how much of $U$ a bump function covers. We could be leaving out points (by mapping them to 0) no matter what bump function we choose.

Given the open cover $W = \set{W_{\alpha} \midline \alpha \in A}$ for $M$ and $\Omega = \set{ \Omega_{\beta} \midline \beta \in B}$ locally finite refinement as above, we can find smooth functions $f_{p} \geq 0$ for every $p \in M$ with $\supp f_{p} \subset \Omega_{\beta} =: \Omega_{p}$.

> We will additionally assume w.l.o.g. that $\Omega_{p} \neq \Omega_{q}$ for $p \neq q$.

The interior of the $\supp f_{p}$ form a  subordinate cover which again has a locally finite refinement. Each element of this is contined in a $\Omega_{p}$ and we can replace it by $\supp f_{p} \subset \Omega_{p}$  since $\Omega$ was already locally finite. Let $P$ denote the set of these points.
We have ensured that $\set{ \supp f_{p} \midline p \in P }$ is locally finite refinement of $W$ and any $q \in M$ lies in atleast one interor of $\supp f_{p}$ for some $p \in B$.

Now we finally define $F := \sum\limits_{p \in P} f_{p}$ does not vanish anywhere and we get a partition of unity

$$
\set{ \frac{f_{p}}{F} \midline p \in P}
$$

`\end{proof}`
