# Doobs Maximal Innequality

We cant to have similar control over $\max \set{X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{n}}$ as we do over a single variable in using Chebishev.

> [!theorem] Doobs Maximal Innequality
> Given a sub-martingale $(X_{n})$ and assume $\Exp{X_{1}} = 0$ then for every $p \geq 1$ and $\gamma > 0$
> $P(\max \set{X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{n}} > \gamma) < \frac{\Exp{|X_{n}|^{p}}}{\gamma^{p}}$

Important here is that we could also consider the absolute value of $(X_{n})$ and simply gain a factor of $2$ in the innequality (becuase we have it once for the positive and once for the negative part.)

Also no assumtions on the $p$-th moment is needed since if its infinite the innequality still holds but just trivially.

`\begin{proof}`
We first write $B = \set{ \max(X_{1}, \ldots, X_{n}) > \gamma}$ and also $T_{\gamma}= \min \set{k \midline X_{k} > \gamma}$. Not that $B$ is composed of the disjoint events $A_{k} = \set{T_{\gamma} = k}$ since one $X_{k}> \gamma$ suffices for the maximum to be aswell.

Now note that we have the following property

$$
\begin{align*}
\Exp{|X_{n}|^{p} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{B} \midline A_{k}} &= \Exp{|X_{n}|^{p} \midline A_{k}} = \frac{\Exp{|X_{n}|^{p} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{A_{k}}}}{P(A_{k})}\\
&= \frac{\Exp{ \Exp{|X_{n}|^{p} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{A_{k}}\midline \mathcal{G_{k}}}}}{P(A_{k})} = \frac{\Exp{ \mathbb{1}_{A_{k}} \Exp{|X_{n}|^{p} \midline \mathcal{G_{k}}}}}{P(A_{k})}
\end{align*}
$$

Now since we have a submartingale we get that $\Exp{|X_{n}|^{p} \midline G_{k}} \geq |X_{k}|^{p} \geq \gamma^{p}$  and thus
$\Exp{|X_{n}|^{p} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{B} \midline A_{k}} \geq \frac{\Exp{ \mathbb{1}_{A_{k}} \gamma^{p}}}{P(A_{k})} = \gamma^{p}$
Since this also gives us that $\Exp{|X_{n}|^{p} \mathbb{1}_{B}\midline B} \geq \gamma^{p}$ we get that
$P(\max \set{X_{1},\ldots, X_{n}}> \gamma) = P(B) \leq \frac{\Exp{|X_{n}|^{p} \mathbb{1}_B}}{\Exp{|X_{n}|^{p} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{B} \midline B}} \leq \frac{\Exp{|X_{n}|^{p}}}{\gamma^{p}}$
`\end{proof}`

As a useful corollary we can now also bound the $L^{p}$ moment of the maximum of any martingale

> [!corollary]
> Fix some $p > 1$ and let $(X_{n})$ be a martingale and assume $\Exp{X_{1}} = 0$. We define $S_{n} = \max \set{X_{k} \midline 1 \leq k \leq n}$ then
> $\Exp{|S_{n}|^{p}} \leq \left( \frac{p}{p-1} \right)^{p} \Exp{|X_{n}|^{p}}$

# Reflection Principle

In the situation of symmetric random variables we can give an even bigger statement that holds

> [!theorem] Reflextion Princple
> Let $(X_{n})$ be i.i.d symmetric random variables and let $S_{n} = \sum\limits_{k=1}^{n} X_{k}$. Then for all $\gamma > 0$ and $n \in \N$ it holds that
> $P(\max(S_{1}, \ldots , S_{n})> \gamma) \leq 2 P(S_{n} > \gamma)$

`\begin{proof}`
This proof is very similar to doobs maximal innequality but we use the extra condition of symmetric random variables to note that
$P \left( \sum\limits_{j=k+1}^{n} X_{j} \geq 0 \right) \geq \frac{1}{2}$
Thus we have that $P(S_{n} > \gamma \mid A_{k} ) \geq \frac{1}{2}$ for any $k$ meaning we have that $P(S_{n} > \gamma \mid B) \geq \frac{1}{2}$ thus giving us that
$P(B) = \frac{}{P(S_{n} > \gamma \mid B)}$

`\end{proof}`
