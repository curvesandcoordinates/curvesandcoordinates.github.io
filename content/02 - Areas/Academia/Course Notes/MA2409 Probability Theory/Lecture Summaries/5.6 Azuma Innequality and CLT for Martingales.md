# Azumas Inequality

A common and useful type of innequality is a concentration inequality, measuring the probability of going away from a specific “center” value such as $X_{0}$

> [!theorem] Azumas Martingale Inequality
> Given a martingale $(X_n)$ wrt to $(\mathcal{G_{n}})$ with a bounded step size, meaning there exists constants $(c_{n})$ such that
> $P(|X_{n} - X_{n-1}| < c_{n}) = 1$
> then we have for every $t > 0$ that
> $P( X_{n} - X_{0} > t) \leq \exp \left( \frac{-t^{2}}{2\sum\limits_{k=1}^{n}c_{k}^{2}} \right)$
> and also
> $P(|X_{n} - X_{0}| > t) \leq 2 \exp \left( \frac{-t^{2}}{2 \sum\limits_{k=1}^{n} c_{k}^{2}} \right)$

`\begin{proof}`
The general strategy for concentration innequalities is to use our simplest one, namely Markov on some random variable. Specifically for some $Y$ we will use the chain
$P(Y > t) = P( e^{\alpha X} > e^{\alpha t}) \leq \frac{\Exp{e^{\alpha X}}}{e^{\alpha t}}$
Where we mainly will setup a good bound for $e^{\alpha X}$

We first define $Z_{n} = X_{n} - X_{n-1}$ and let $(U_{n})$ be i.i.d be a uniform $[0,1]$ independent of the entire filtration $(\mathcal{G_{n}})$. Let $W_{n}$ be defined as follows:
$W_{n} = \begin{cases} c_{n} && U_{n} < \frac{Z_{n} + c_{n}}{2c_{n}} \\ -c_{n} && otherwise \end{cases}$

`\end{proof}`
