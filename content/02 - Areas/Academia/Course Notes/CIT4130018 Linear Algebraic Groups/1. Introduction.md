In this course we consider for a given algebraically closed field $k$ the general linear group and its connected Family. We note that this object is in the sense of algebraic geometry an affine algebraic variety since it is described by the polynomial equation
$\det A = 0$
At the same time while we can describe it as a Zariski-Open subset of $\A^{n \times n}$.

We thus concern ourselves in general with _affine algebraic varieties_ with an added _group structure_. This is close to Lie groups since if we were to consider simply just affine varieties with group structure we would be in the Lie group setting.

It turns out that the known example of $GL_{n, k}$ is not just a useful example but it encompasses all the example of linear algebraic groups. We thus aim to categorise and classify every linear algebraic group we can.
