This chapter is mainly a recap of topics taught in Algebra 2.

We consider a commutative Ring $R$ with $1$ and let $\spec R$ be the set of all prime ideals of $R$. We can then further formally define the [[Zariski Topology]] and describe the connection described by [[Hilbert’s Nullstellensatz]] between affine $k$-algebras and affine sets (also known as zero sets).
