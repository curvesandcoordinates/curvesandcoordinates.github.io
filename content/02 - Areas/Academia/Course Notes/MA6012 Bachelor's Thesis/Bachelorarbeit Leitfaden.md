_Supervisor:_ [[Bachelorbeteruer Anschreiben#Michael Marc Wolf Differential Forms]]
_Topic:_ “Topology of Unitary Groups”

In general we are interested in a couple of topological Properties like

- Path-Connectedness
- Betti Numbers
  of the Lie Group $SU(n) = \set{M \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n} \mid MM^{*} = \mathbb{1}, \det(M) = 1}$

Interestingly we have a couple of simple examples where our question is already solved:

- $SU(1) = \set{(1)} \cong \set{e^{0}}$
- $SU(2) \cong S^{3}$
