In this chapter we consider the topological properties of specifically $U(n)$ and $SU(n)$, following [[Rotation Matrix Groups#Topological Properties]] finding that these are compact and path connected [[Lie Groups - Manifolds + Groups|lie groups]].

We also prove that one can always decompose the [[Rotation Matrix Groups#Decomposing $U(n)$|unitary matrices]] via
$U(n) \cong SU(n) \times U(1)$
which allows us to limit ourselves to $SU(n)$ and simply always extend the calculated [[De Rahm Cohomology]] in [[4. Spectral Sequences|chapter 4]].

Next we describe the rich structure of $SU(n)$ as a [[Fibre Bundles|fibre bundle]]. Namely we claim the result that
$\faktor{SU(n)}{SU(n-1)} \cong \mathbb{S}^{n-1}$
which is very useful in later chapters.
