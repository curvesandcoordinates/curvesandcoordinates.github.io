In the Introduction of the thesis we mainly focus on defining the [[Rotation Matrix Groups|unitary matrix groups]] explicitly, mainly following the seperate note for this. This construction allows the reader to know what we are talking about.

A central role is the characterization of $O(2)$ by explicit rotation matrices. This shows us that this group can be parametrized in some way by one angle and motivates studying these structures as manifolds in [[2. Topological Properties|chapter 2]]. The parametrization has a periodicity which expreses that the structure of $O(2)$ has some “perodicity as well” later shown in [[4. Spectral Sequences|chapter 4]] via the betti numbers.
