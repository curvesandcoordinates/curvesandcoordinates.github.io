Conclusively, we have shown that $U(n)$ and $SU(n)$ are path-connected compact Lie groups. Their Betti numbers are compactly represented by the Poincaré polynomials

$$$\begin{aligned}
    P_{U(n)}(t) &= \prod_{k = 1}^n (1 + t^{2k-1}) \\
    P_{SU(n)}(t) &= \prod_{k = 2}^n (1 + t^{2k-1})
\end{aligned}$$ and allow for computation of their Euler-Characteristic, which is given by $\chi(U(n)) = \chi(SU(n)) = 0$.
