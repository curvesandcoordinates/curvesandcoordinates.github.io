We can consider a parameter space of rights, in which each coordinate describes how much a certrain freedom or right is guaranteed to the citizens of a state. Then the current politcal positions of any state can be represented as one point in this state space and thus we can consider the dynamics of this system.

Interesting questions here might be if we have general equilibria that are stable or unstable (see for example the [[What is the Worth of Privacy - Why 1984 is good!|Privacy Equilibrium]]) or can we not assume general equilibria.

Maybe historically speaking states rotate in a specific cycle giving us a hegalian view of history, saying it is doomed to repeat itself. Im not sure if Ive ever looked into this.
