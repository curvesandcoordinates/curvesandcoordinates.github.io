# History

Historically speaking classical algeraic geometry is the study of zero sets of polynomials. These correspond to geometric objects, some of which one might have seen in school in form of a graph. In general when studying the interplay between properties of the polynomial and the corresponding geometric properties of the representing object we get to delve into many different properties algebraic topics such as ring theory or module theory. In fact many of the properties we care about in these areas are motivated purely by the geometric idea behind them.

Centrally we also want to define a rigid translation mechanism between algebra and geometry. This has multiple paths to it, two of which are mainly used and occured at different times.
