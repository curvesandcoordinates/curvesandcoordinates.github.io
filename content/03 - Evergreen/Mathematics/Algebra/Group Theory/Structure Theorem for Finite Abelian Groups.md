Which gets us to the big theorem for finite [[Groups|groups]]

> [!theorem|2.20] Structure Theorem for Finite Abelian Groups
> Given a finite Abelian group $G$ there exist integers $r_{1}, \ldots, r_{k} \in \N$ such that
>
> 1. $r_{j} \mid r_{j-1}$ for all $j$
> 2. $|G| = r_{1} \cdot r_{2} \cdots r_{k}$
> 3. $G \cong \Z_{r_{1}} \times \ldots \times \Z_{r_{k}}$

This give us that we can without loss of generality restrict our theory to the cartesian products of finite [[Cyclic Groups|cyclic groups]].
