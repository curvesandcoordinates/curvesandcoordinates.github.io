> [!example|\*] Logarithmic Spiral
> Consider $\gamma:\N \to \R^{2} \cong \C, \quad \gamma_{k} = e^{k(-\epsilon + i \delta)}$
> for $\epsilon, \delta > 0$. This is really just a discretisation of a smooth logarithmic spiral.
> We can calculate the arc-length of this discrete curve and find that it is finite alrhough the curve is defined on an infinite (discrete) interval.
> For this calculation ==which will be done tomorrow== consider
> $\Delta \gamma_{k} = \gamma_{k+1} - \gamma_{k} = e^{(k+1)(-\epsilon + i \delta)}- e^{k(-\epsilon + i \delta)} = e^{k(-\epsilon + i \delta)} (1 - e^{-\epsilon + i \delta})...$
