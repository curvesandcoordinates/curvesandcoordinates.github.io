Identify $z \in \C$ with $\lambda \begin{pmatrix}z \\ 1\end{pmatrix} \subset \C^{2}$ and $\infty$ with $\begin{pmatrix}\lambda \\ 0\end{pmatrix} \subset \C^{2}$.

We can identify [[Möbius Transformations]] with matrices in $SL_{2}(\C)$ by considering
$\begin{pmatrix}a & b \\ c & d\end{pmatrix}$
then this maps our homogeneous coordinates by
$\begin{pmatrix}a & b \\ c & d\end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix}z \\ 1\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}az + b \\ cz + d\end{pmatrix} \cong \begin{pmatrix}\frac{a z + b}{c z + d} \\ 1\end{pmatrix}$
