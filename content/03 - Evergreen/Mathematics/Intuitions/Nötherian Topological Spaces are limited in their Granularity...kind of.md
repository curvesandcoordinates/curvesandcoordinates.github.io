Considering the usual definition of a noetherian topological space, we could try and intuitively consider it as a limit on granularity, but the problem is that is isnt a specific numerical limit.

The one way we can think of a Space being Nötherian is using the fact if our space is Hausdorff (meaning if we can intuitively separate points) we know that the space must be finite.

The image you can keep in mind is that when considering nötherianess we consider chains of closed subsets, which you can imagine as strings. Our nötherian property gives us that these strings must have an end but not how they are structured. Hausdorfness gives us a way to say that all strands can be separated from one another.
