## Content

Many people think of Groups as Permutations of Elements or in a more geometric sense the Symmetries of a Geometric object like a Square, Triangle etc.
When I learned of groups I learned - briefly - about [Cayley’s Theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cayley%27s_theorem) which allows us to think of all finite Groups as Subgroups of a Symmetric Group. This allows us to try and connect a given Subgroup of the Symmetric Group to an Object and thus connect every finite given group to an Object of Symmetry.

Via Frucht’s Theorem we can assosciate every group of symmetries with the automorphisms of an undirected graph. If we embedd this graph $G = (V, E)$ into $\mathbb{R}^{|V|}$ with associating general $g$ with $e_{i}$ in no particular order. We can then just fill this in, and get a Polytope with the edges being basis vectors. This construction isnt special, but it lets us construct a general Object whose symmetries are group connected to group elements
