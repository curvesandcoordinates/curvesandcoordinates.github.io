### Description / Trigger

I watched this video [Visiting the Fattest, Most Cigarette-Addicted and Least Visited Country](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eUJgq9HerDQ) and thought about the scary development the island of Nauru went through.
It gives me feelings that I had in zanzibar where a underdeveloped but slightly “clean” society gets exposed to all the good and bad of advanced technology at once and simply cant handle it.

### My thoughts so far:

- What type of development do developed areas (especially wealthy ones) go through?
- Is this process like an Anthropological disease or disorder caused by “shor-sightedness” of humans?
  - If so how can we work around this to still have nice things?
- Are we experience micro-forms of this development everytime a new technology (think AI or YT Shorts) gets introduced and simply have to learn to cope first (like an immune system)
