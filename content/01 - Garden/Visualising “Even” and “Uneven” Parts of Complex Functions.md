## Idea

When we consider the Definition of Sine and Cosine of the Exponential Function we learn that because of

$$\begin{align*}
\sin(z) = \frac{e^{iz} - e^{-iz}}{2i}\\
\cos(z) = \frac{e^{iz} + e^{-iz}}{2i} 
\end{align*}
$$

the sine can be seen as the even and the cosine as the odd part of the exponential function.

- How can this be Generalised?
- Is there a rotational symmetry between even and odd parts of functions?
- Can you "see" the even and odd parts of functions?
