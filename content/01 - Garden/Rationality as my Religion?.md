## Personal “Religion”

I personally could claim that one of the biggest principles I form my opinions by is the law of non-contradiction. This is a simple logical rule stating that a statement “A” and the negated statement “not A” cant be true. Taking this as an assumption my world view while not always avoiding contradiction attempts to uphold this law.
