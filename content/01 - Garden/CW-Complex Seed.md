### Why I’m interested
The point of CW complexes is that we construct combinatorially simple spaces to approximate general topological spaces. I’ve never understood the construction explicitly though even when getting it explained by conrad. It might be worth to look into as they are a good example to consider homology.

### Possible next steps
- Read Hatcher on CW Complexes (or some other topology book)
- Work though an example calculating homology or cohomology for CW Complexes to see their strength
